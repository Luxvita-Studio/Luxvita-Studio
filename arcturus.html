Arcturus

ARCTURUS is a creative initiative founded by our close collaborators, built around a shared fascination with astronomy and the stories hidden beyond the night sky. Through photography and filmmaking, they explore the universe not only as something to be observed, but as something that reminds us how small, and yet how curious we are.

Our relationship with ARCTURUS grew from a simple exchange of ideas into a collaboration between two creative paths. While LUXVITA approaches the world through visual storytelling, ARCTURUS looks beyond it, toward the skies above. Together, we share a common ground in exploration, documentation, and the desire to see things from a different perspective.

ARCTURUS is not a part of LUXVITA, but a companion along the way. Our first collaboration, Ekspedisi Pulang Kepada Alam, became a meeting point between these two perspectives, bringing together our shared curiosity, love for exploration, and passion for visual storytelling.

Different paths. One curiosity. Something beyond what we can see.
